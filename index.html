ABOUT OUR PLATFORM

Everything about Comments Viewer

Comments Viewer is a simple and interactive platform where users can read, organize, post, and save comments.

🔍

Explore Comments

Browse comments collected from an online API. Comments are organized into different categories such as Technology, Sports, Education, Movies, Travel, Health, Business, and General.

📝

Post Your Thoughts

Users can write their own comments by entering their username, email, category, and message. Posted comments are stored in the browser.

❤️

Save Favorites

If you like a comment, click the favorite button. The comment will be stored in your Favorites page even after refreshing the browser.

🔐

Simple Account System

Users can create an account and log in. Account details and session information are stored locally in the browser using LocalStorage.
